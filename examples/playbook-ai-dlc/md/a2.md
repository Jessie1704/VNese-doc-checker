# A.2. Số stage của từng profile {#c-a2}

| `classic` | 18 | 33 | Standard | relaxed | off | advisory | - |
| `enterprise` | 33 | 33 | Comprehensive | strict | on | theo stage | - |
| `express` | 10 | 33 | Minimal | relaxed | off | none | `/aidlc-express` |
